# Supporting-workflow task

Build one original task in the support domain.

- Applications: choose two or three admitted assets that can score a support workflow (a
  help-desk ticket, a CRM contact, a mailbox). At least one application must carry a scored
  terminal effect.
- The public request is a short work instruction from a business colleague. It refers to "our
  current procedure" and points at a readable record holding the rules; it must not state the
  decisive values itself.
- The world holds one plausible lookalike record that is legitimately out of scope, with a
  discoverable business reason for leaving it alone.
- The task ends with one notification to a named recipient, and the scored assertions are state
  predicates over real records.
- Provide the narrowest reference path: the API calls that reach the correct terminal state.
